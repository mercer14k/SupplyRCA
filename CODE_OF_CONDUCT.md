# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Welcome domain and engineering critique focused on evidence. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish others' private information, or pressure contributors for personal details. Assume good intent while taking reports of harm seriously.

Maintain constructive discussions: explain the operational context, distinguish evidence from opinion, acknowledge uncertainty and give actionable feedback. This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and project-related events.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers using the contact route on the published repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not repeat sensitive details in a public thread. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation or exclude repeat/serious violators. They should disclose conflicts of interest and document decisions privately. Appeals may be sent through the same private channel.
